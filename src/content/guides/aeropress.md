title: AeroPress
description: The most forgiving brewer there is — one tool, endless recipes, great coffee anywhere.
method: aeropress
tagline: Versatile, portable, and easy to learn.
---

The AeroPress brews by steeping coffee and then pressing it through a paper filter. It forgives almost any mistake: too strong is fixed with hot water, too weak with a longer steep. It's the best first brewer for learning how grind size and time change the cup.
