---
title: French Press
description: Full-bodied immersion brewing with almost no technique required.
method: french-press
tagline: Full-bodied and simple to get right.
---

The French press steeps coffee fully immersed in water, giving a heavy, rounded body. Use a coarse grind, steep four minutes, skim the crust, and press gently. Pour it all out at once — coffee left on the grounds keeps extracting and turns bitter.
