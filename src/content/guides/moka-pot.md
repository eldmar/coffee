title: Moka Pot
description: The classic stovetop brewer — strong, syrupy coffee with a little care.
method: moka-pot
tagline: Classic stovetop and strong.
---

The moka pot pushes steam-pressured water up through a bed of coffee. Start with hot water in the base, keep the heat low, and take it off the stove as soon as the flow turns pale and starts to sputter — that's the difference between rich and burnt.
