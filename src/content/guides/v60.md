The V60's spiral cone and large opening give you full control over the brew — which also means technique matters. Keep your pours slow and circular, keep the coffee bed flat, and let a scale with a timer be your honest coach.

## Troubleshooting

**Bitter or drying cup.** You extracted too much. Grind coarser, lower the water temperature a few degrees, or pour more gently — aggressive pouring stirs up fines that slow the drawdown and over-extract.

**Sour or weak cup.** The opposite: grind finer, or check the brew didn't rush through. A total time under 2:30 for 250 ml usually means the grind is too coarse.

**Drawdown stalls.** If the bed takes far longer than 3:30 to drain, the grind is too fine or your pours flooded the bed with fines. Coarsen slightly and pour in slow, low circles.

**Muddy, flat flavors.** Rinse the paper filter properly before brewing, and check your water — heavily chlorinated tap water flattens everything a good bean can offer.
