## Steps

1. Rinse the paper filter, assemble the AeroPress on your cup, and add the coffee.
2. Pour in all the water, start the timer, and give it three gentle stirs.
3. Insert the plunger to seal, and wait until 1:30.
4. Press slowly for about 30 seconds, stopping at the hiss.

## Tips

- Water just off the boil works, but 85–90 °C makes darker roasts noticeably sweeter.
- Too intense? Just top up with hot water — an instant americano-style cup.
