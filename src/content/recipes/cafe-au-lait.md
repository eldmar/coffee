Café au lait is brewed coffee and hot milk in roughly equal measure. There is no espresso and almost no foam — it is the gentlest way to drink coffee with milk, and the reason French breakfast bowls exist.

## Steps

1. Heat the filtered water to 92–96 °C.
2. Grind the coffee to a medium consistency.
3. Brew 12 g of coffee with 200 g of water in approximately 3–4 minutes.
4. Heat the milk to 60–65 °C without bringing it to a boil.
5. Create only a small amount of foam — the milk should remain mostly liquid.
6. Pour approximately 180 g of brewed coffee into the serving vessel.
7. Add an equal amount of hot milk and serve.

## Barista tip

Café au lait is made with brewed coffee, not espresso. Keep the coffee and milk close to a 1:1 ratio.

## Troubleshooting

**The drink tastes too milky.** Brew the coffee slightly stronger or reduce the milk.

**The milk tastes cooked.** Keep it below 70 °C.

**The coffee tastes weak.** Grind slightly finer or extend the brewing time.
