## Steps

1. Preheat a 150–180 ml cappuccino cup.
2. Pull a 36–40 g double espresso into the cup.
3. Pour 120–150 ml of cold milk into a pitcher.
4. Introduce air for the first few seconds, then keep the steam tip just below the surface to build a whirlpool.
5. Stop at approximately 60–65°C and swirl until the milk looks glossy and uniform.
6. Pour steadily, keeping the espresso, warm milk and microfoam balanced throughout the cup.

## Classic Cappuccino Ratio and Size

The traditional shorthand is equal thirds of espresso, steamed milk and foam. In practice, use a 36–40 g double espresso with enough textured milk to finish at 150–180 ml. The exact layers should merge into one velvety drink rather than sit as three rigid bands.

## The Right Milk Texture

Cappuccino microfoam should be glossy, fine and noticeably more aerated than flat-white milk, without large visible bubbles. It should pour rather than fall from the pitcher in stiff spoonfuls. Whole milk gives the most forgiving texture; barista oat milk is a reliable dairy-free alternative.

## Cappuccino vs Latte

A [Caffè Latte](/recipes/caffe-latte/) is larger, milkier and topped with a thinner layer of microfoam. A cappuccino uses less milk in a smaller cup, so the espresso tastes stronger and the foam texture is more prominent.

## Cappuccino vs Flat White

A [Flat White](/recipes/flat-white/) has a thinner, silkier microfoam layer and usually a higher coffee-to-milk ratio. Cappuccino milk contains more incorporated air and gives the drink a lighter, fuller texture.

## Common Milk-Steaming Mistakes

- **Large bubbles:** the steam tip stayed too high above the milk. Lower it slightly and add air more gently.
- **Thin, flat milk:** not enough air was introduced at the start. Stretch the milk for a few seconds before creating the whirlpool.
- **Dry, stiff foam:** too much air was added or the milk sat before pouring. Add less air and swirl immediately.
- **Scalded flavour:** the milk became too hot. Stop steaming at 60–65°C.
- **Foam separates in the cup:** the pitcher was not swirled before pouring. Recombine the milk and microfoam until glossy.
