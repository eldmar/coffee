## Steps

1. Add the coffee to the jar, pour in the cold water, and stir until every ground is wet.
2. Cover and steep in the fridge for 14–16 hours.
3. Strain through a paper filter or fine sieve into a clean bottle.
4. Serve over ice, diluted with water or milk to taste — the concentrate is strong.

## Tips

- Keeps up to a week in the fridge, so brew a big batch on Sunday.
- Coarse grind is essential; fine grounds make it muddy and bitter.
