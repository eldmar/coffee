*Macchiato* means "marked". The drink is espresso with just enough milk to soften the edge — a spoonful, not a splash. It should still taste unmistakably of coffee.

## Steps

1. Preheat a 60–90 ml demitasse.
2. Grind 18 g of coffee and brew a double espresso, aiming for 36–40 g in 25–30 seconds.
3. Pour 30–40 ml of cold milk into a small pitcher — far less than feels sensible.
4. Steam briefly to about 55 °C, adding almost no air. You want a wet, glossy texture, not foam.
5. Spoon or pour one to two tablespoons of milk onto the centre of the shot, leaving a pale mark in the crema.
6. Serve immediately, before the mark spreads.

## Recommended ratio

Roughly four parts espresso to one part milk. If the milk covers the surface, you have made a very small cortado.

## Espresso macchiato vs latte macchiato

They are near opposites. An espresso macchiato is coffee marked with milk. A latte macchiato is a glass of milk marked with coffee — much larger, much milder, and usually layered.

## Tips

- Steaming this little milk is easier in the smallest pitcher you own.
- If your pitcher cannot texture 40 ml, steam a bit more and use only the best of it.
- The mark fades within a minute; this is a drink to make and hand over, not to photograph at length.
