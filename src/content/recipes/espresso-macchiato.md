*Macchiato* means "marked". The espresso stays dominant and the milk is an accent, not the main ingredient.

## Steps

1. Preheat the demitasse with hot water, then dry it.
2. Grind and dose 18 g of coffee into the portafilter.
3. Distribute evenly and tamp level.
4. Pull 36 g of espresso in approximately 25–30 seconds.
5. Steam the milk to create glossy microfoam, stopping at 55–60 °C.
6. Add one spoonful of microfoam to the centre of the espresso, allowing only a small amount of liquid milk into the cup.
7. Serve immediately.

## Barista tip

The espresso should remain dominant. Steaming this little milk is easier in the smallest pitcher you own — texture 100 g and use only the best of it.

## Troubleshooting

**It tastes like a small cappuccino.** Use less milk.

**The foam is dry or stiff.** Introduce less air and swirl the pitcher before serving.

**The espresso tastes sharp.** Grind slightly finer or increase the extraction time.

## Espresso macchiato vs latte macchiato

They are near opposites. An espresso macchiato is coffee marked with milk. A latte macchiato is a glass of milk marked with coffee — much larger, much milder, and usually layered.
