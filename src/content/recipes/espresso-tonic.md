## Steps

1. Fill a tall glass to the top with ice and pour in the chilled tonic.
2. Pull a double shot into a small pitcher.
3. Pour the espresso slowly over the back of a spoon so it floats on the tonic.
4. Garnish with citrus and serve with a straw — stir just before drinking.

## Tips

- Fruity, light-roast beans shine here; dark roasts can clash with the quinine bitterness.
- Chill the glass and the tonic well — temperature contrast is the whole point.
