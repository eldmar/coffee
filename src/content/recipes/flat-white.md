## Steps

1. Pull a double shot (18 g in, about 36 g out in 25–30 seconds) into a 160 ml cup.
2. Steam the milk to 55–60 °C, aiming for a glossy microfoam with no visible bubbles.
3. Swirl the pitcher, then pour from low height, finishing with a thin white dot or rosetta.
4. Serve immediately — a flat white is best while the foam is still tight.

## Tips

- If the shot tastes sour, grind slightly finer; if bitter, grind coarser.
- Whole milk textures best, but oat drink in "barista" versions works well too.
