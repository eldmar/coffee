A freddo espresso is a double shot shaken hard with ice until it turns cold and grows a fine, pale foam. It stays black, stays strong, and takes about a minute longer than an espresso.

## Steps

1. Fill the serving glass with fresh ice.
2. Grind and dose 18 g of coffee into the portafilter.
3. Distribute evenly and tamp level.
4. Pull 36–40 g of espresso in approximately 25–30 seconds.
5. If using sugar, dissolve it in the hot espresso.
6. Transfer the espresso to a shaker with two or three ice cubes.
7. Shake firmly for 10–12 seconds, until chilled and lightly foamed.
8. Strain over the fresh ice and serve immediately.

## Barista tip

Use separate ice for shaking and serving. The first batch chills the espresso; the fresh ice keeps the finished drink from becoming watery.

## Troubleshooting

**The drink tastes watery.** Shake for less time or use larger ice cubes.

**There is no foam.** Shake more vigorously while the espresso is still hot.

**The coffee tastes harsh.** Adjust the espresso extraction before adding more sugar.
