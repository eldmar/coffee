## Steps

1. Drizzle caramel sauce around the inside of a tall glass and fill it with ice.
2. Pour in the cold milk, leaving room at the top.
3. Pull a double shot and pour it slowly over the milk for a layered look.
4. Stir before drinking, adding an extra caramel drizzle on top if you like.

## Tips

- Chill the glass first so the ice melts slower and the drink stays strong.
- No machine? A double moka pot brew poured over ice works nicely.
