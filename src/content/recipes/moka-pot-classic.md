## Steps

1. Fill the base with hot (not cold) water up to the safety valve.
2. Fill the basket with coffee, level it off — never tamp.
3. Assemble and place on low heat with the lid open.
4. When the flow turns pale and starts to sputter, take it off the heat and serve.

## Tips

- Starting with hot water keeps the grounds from cooking while the pot heats up.
- Rinse with warm water only, no soap — and dry fully before storing.
