## Steps

1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water and discard the rinse water.
2. Add 15 g of coffee, level the bed, and start your timer as you pour.
3. Bloom with 45 ml of water and wait 30 seconds.
4. Pour in slow circles to 150 g by 1:15, then to 250 g by 2:00.
5. Total brew time should land around 2:45–3:15. Swirl, let it drain, and serve.

## Tips

- If the brew runs much faster than 2:45, grind finer; much slower than 3:30, grind coarser.
- Use filtered water — with pour over, water quality is half the cup.
